## Grading

Both images receive the identical deterministic Pillow process in `grade.py`, run with `python3`. Dimensions stay 1536 × 1024, with no resizing or cropping. Source color is discarded by computing luminance as (0.2126 R + 0.7152 G + 0.0722 B) / 255. A gentle luminance lift uses exponent 0.92. Fine independent monochrome grain is then added in luminance, uniform ±0.02 of full scale (about ±5 levels in 8-bit; RMS about 1.15%), using seeds 6081 and 6082.

The grained luminance is clamped to [0,1] and mapped through a 65,536-entry continuous warm tritone lookup table: #14120F at 0, a midtone at 0.46 blending 90% #6E6759 warm stone with 10% #8A6425 gold-brown, and #FDF6EC at 1. Each segment interpolates RGB linearly. Gold is deliberately restrained to avoid saturated orange. Applying grain before palette mapping keeps all output colors on this warm palette path, rather than adding colored noise. RGB ordering R ≥ G ≥ B is verified for every pixel. Outputs are RGB PNGs, `graded-1.png` and `graded-2.png`.
