## Grading

The raw PNGs are untouched generated image bytes. Both are 1536 × 1024. `grade.py` uses Pillow via `python3`. It converts to luminance and maps piecewise linearly through #14120F at 0%, #6E6759 at 48%, #8A6425 at 67%, and #FDF6EC at 100%. Every output color is an interpolation along this prescribed warm ramp. Fine independent monochrome grain is applied to luminance before mapping, uniform ±2% of full scale, with deterministic seeds 7011 and 7012. Values are clamped before mapping; no colored noise, resizing, cropping, sharpening, or other edits. Graded files are RGB PNGs at the original resolution.
